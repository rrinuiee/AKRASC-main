About AKRASC

Innovate. Integrate. Inspire.

AKRASC is the flagship event of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) Kerala Chapter, designed to bring together the brightest young minds in the field. This conclave serves as a dynamic platform for students to showcase their talents, learn from leading experts, and network with peers and industry professionals. Through a series of workshops, competitions, and talks, we aim to foster a culture of innovation and collaboration in robotics and automation across Kerala.
