ABOUT AKRASC

AKRASC, the All Kerala Robotics and Automation Society Conclave, presented by the IEEE RAS Kerala Chapter, is a premier series of events focused on the ever-evolving world of robotics and automation. Curated and led by trailblazers in the field, AKRASC brings together passionate minds—from curious students to emerging professionals—for an immersive experience in cutting-edge technologies.

Each session dives deep into the latest innovations, research breakthroughs, and real-world applications in robotics and automation, offering participants both technical insights and inspiration. AKRASC isn't just about learning—it's about building a collaborative community that drives forward-thinking ideas and empowers the next generation of tech leaders.
